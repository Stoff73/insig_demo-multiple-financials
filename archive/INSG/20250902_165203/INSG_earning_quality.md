## 2. Forensic Accounting Metrics Analysis Summary

### Valuation Ratios

The EV/EBIT ratio at 0.0x is misleading, caused by near-zero or negative EBIT, signaling no operational earnings to justify current valuation. EV/Revenue at 42.0x is extremely high, indicating the market prices the company on growth hopes rather than current fundamentals. Free cash flow yield at 0.0% indicates no free cash generation, consistent with reported cash burn. These valuation metrics appear inflated and unsupported by sustainable fundamentals.

### Profitability

Gross margin is reported at 100%, which is unusual and potentially reflects accounting policies or lack of cost of goods sold, which warrants careful review. Operating margin at -567.3% and net margin at negative -4307.7% show extraordinarily poor profitability, indicative of a non-repeatable loss profile. The returns (ROE -2031.3%, ROCE -157.2%) indicate extreme destruction of shareholder value with no signs of operational leverage. These metrics confirm severe operational and profitability distress.

### Liquidity

Current ratio and quick ratio stand at a dangerously low 0.1, far below thresholds for operational liquidity, indicating the company is unable to meet its short-term obligations with available assets, posing a material going concern risk.

### Leverage

Debt-to-equity at 1.0 is at the monitoring threshold, reflecting significant indebtedness relative to equity. Interest coverage of -16.6x negative indicates the company is unable to service debt interest from operating earnings, signifying high financial risk.

### Efficiency and Earnings Quality

Inventory turnover of 0.0x likely reflects the company's business model (possibly no inventory) or accounting. Days sales outstanding at 38 days is adequate and indicates competent receivables management. Accruals ratio of -686.8% suggests minimal accrual manipulation and more cash-based earnings components, passing quality checks. However, EBITDA to FCF conversion at 0.0 indicates EBITDA is not translating to cash, highlighting poor earnings quality and cash burn. Adjusted vs statutory gap is negligible, suggesting reporting consistency.

### Asset Quality

There is no goodwill on the balance sheet, eliminating risks associated with goodwill impairment. Capex is zero relative to depreciation, suggesting no reinvestment or asset additions, warranting monitoring over asset sustainability. Tangible book value is negative £-2.8m, signaling liabilities exceeding net tangible assets, a major weakness on the balance sheet.

### Cash Flow

Cash conversion ratio near zero or negative corroborates poor cash flow quality. Free cash flow is negative £-1.3m, confirming the company consumes cash rather than generates it operationally.

## 3. Ownership and Governance Considerations

Insig AI’s ownership is concentrated with significant insider holdings: Director Richard Bernstein controls 18.6% of shares and holds share options, signaling a strong control stake. Other directors and key shareholders hold meaningful stakes above 3%. However, recent management changes—including the passing of John Murray and resignations of senior directors—indicate potential governance instability. Combined with severe financial distress noted above, this concentration of ownership could have mixed implications: potentially strong leadership versus risk of entrenchment or governance challenges.

## 6. Synthesis and Conclusion

The comprehensive forensic review reveals Insig AI as a company in severe financial distress, with unsustainable losses, poor liquidity, negative cash flows, and a weak balance sheet with negative tangible equity. Profitability metrics are deeply negative and not repeatable, while the valuation multiples are inflated by market expectations rather than fundamentals. The accounting notes confirm the company’s losses are compounded by significant intangible impairments and complex revenue recognition on milestone basis, potentially smoothing revenue recognition but not improving cash generation.

Ownership concentration with a dominant insider and recent director turnover raises governance risks in this fragile context. The auditor highlights material uncertainty on going concern, and the CEO admits operational revenue challenges despite strategic initiatives.

Investors should consider the high risk profile due to cash burn, liquidity stress, and the dependency on future capital raises. Earnings quality is poor, with EBITDA not translating into free cash flow and negative accrual effects absent. The company’s prospects depend critically on operational turnaround, capital funding, and execution of strategic growth initiatives.
