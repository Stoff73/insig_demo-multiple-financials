**Ownership & Governance:** No explicit ownership percentages or major shareholders were disclosed in the notes. Subsidiary ownership details are provided, but insider ownership and control influence cannot be assessed fully.

Spire Healthcare demonstrates solid operational performance with strong revenue growth and gross margins.
