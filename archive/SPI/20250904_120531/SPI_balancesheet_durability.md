Operating margin (9.4%) and net margin (1.7%) are moderate and flagged as monitor, indicating some pressure on profitability. Return on Equity and Return on Capital Employed are below pass thresholds, suggesting modest returns and room for improvement.

**Liquidity:** The current ratio (0.7) and quick ratio (0.5) are below pass thresholds, indicating potential liquidity risk or tight working capital management.

**Leverage:** Debt-to-equity ratio is high at 1.7, interest coverage is low at 0.7x, and net debt to EBITDA is 5.0x, all failing thresholds, indicating significant leverage and potential risk in servicing debt.

**Cash Flow:** Cash conversion ratio is very strong at 9.3, and free cash flow is positive at £123.6m, confirming strong cash generation.

**Executive Remuneration and Governance:** Executive pay is aligned with performance, with moderate bonus payouts and LTIP vesting outcomes reflecting balanced reward for achieved performance. The remuneration structure is transparent and linked to financial and strategic goals, supporting management alignment with shareholders.
