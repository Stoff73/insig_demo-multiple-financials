## Analysis Summary

**Valuation Ratios:** XP Power's valuation multiples such as EV/EBITDA and EV/EBIT at 67.6x are significantly above acceptable thresholds, indicating the stock is priced at a premium relative to earnings. Price to free cash flow is also low at 3.7x, reinforcing the overvaluation concern. However, the free cash flow yield of 26.9% is a positive sign, suggesting the company generates strong cash relative to its market price. This disparity may reflect market optimism about future growth or turnaround potential, but current earnings do not justify the valuation.

**Profitability:** The company exhibits weak profitability metrics. Gross margin is slightly below the preferred threshold at 39.2%, operating margin is very low at 1.5%, and net margin is negative at -3.9%. Returns on equity and capital employed are negative or near zero, indicating poor operational efficiency and profitability. This aligns with the income statement showing adjusted operating profit of £4.8m on revenue of £110.9m and a net loss after finance costs.

**Liquidity:** The current ratio of 1.6 indicates adequate short-term liquidity, while the quick ratio of 0.9 is borderline but still acceptable. This suggests the company can meet its short-term obligations but with limited liquid asset buffer.

**Leverage:** Debt-to-equity ratio of 1.1 and net debt to EBITDA of 24.5x are very high, signaling significant leverage risk. Interest coverage ratio of 0.2x shows the company struggles to cover interest expenses from operating earnings, a major financial risk. The notes reveal the company has a $140 million revolving credit facility with £78 million drawn, and covenant amendments requiring leverage below 3.0x and interest cover above 3.0x, which the company currently does not meet comfortably.

**Efficiency:** Inventory turnover of 4.2x and days sales outstanding of 22 days are strong, indicating efficient working capital management and good receivables collection.

**Earnings Quality:** The accruals ratio is negative at -31.2%, indicating conservative accounting. EBITDA to free cash flow conversion is very high at 1158%, showing strong cash flow quality relative to earnings. The adjusted vs statutory earnings gap is zero, indicating no significant earnings manipulation.

**Asset Quality:** Goodwill to assets ratio is moderate at 17.6%, and tangible book value is solid at £72.7m. However, the capex to depreciation ratio is zero, which is unusual and flagged for monitoring, possibly indicating underinvestment or timing differences in capital expenditure.

**Cash Flow:** Cash conversion ratio of 5.8x and positive free cash flow of £35.3m support liquidity and debt servicing capacity, which is positive amid weak profitability.

**Ownership Structure:** The company has a high free float (~95%) with major institutional shareholders holding about 28% combined. This ownership profile supports good governance and market transparency.

**Auditor's Report:** The auditor's review report dated 5 August 2025 expresses no material concerns or qualifications and confirms the interim financial information is prepared in accordance with IAS 34. This supports the reliability of the reported financials.

## Conclusion

XP Power faces significant challenges in profitability and financial leverage. Despite strong cash flow generation and efficient working capital management, the company is highly leveraged with poor interest coverage and negative net margins. Valuation multiples are inflated relative to earnings and cash flow, suggesting market optimism not yet supported by fundamentals.

The strong free cash flow and cash conversion ratios provide some cushion and flexibility, and the company has amended its credit facilities with covenant headroom. However, the zero capex to depreciation ratio is a concern for sustainable asset maintenance and future operational capacity.

Investors should be cautious and monitor improvements in operating margins, debt reduction, and interest coverage before considering the stock undervalued or a safe investment.
