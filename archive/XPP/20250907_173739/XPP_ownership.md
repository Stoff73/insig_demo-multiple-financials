## Analysis Summary

XP Power is characterized by a strong institutional ownership base with a high free-float, which supports liquidity and market participation. The major shareholders are reputable investment firms, indicating confidence in the company’s long-term prospects despite current challenges.

Financially, XP Power is under pressure with weak profitability metrics: operating margin (1.5%) and net margin (-3.9%) are poor, and returns on equity and capital employed are negative or very low. The company’s leverage is high, with debt-to-equity at 1.1 and net debt to EBITDA at 24.5x, alongside very weak interest coverage (0.2x), signaling financial risk.

However, liquidity ratios are adequate, with a current ratio of 1.6 and a quick ratio near 1. Efficiency in working capital management is good, as shown by inventory turnover and days sales outstanding. Earnings quality is strong, with excellent EBITDA to free cash flow conversion and low accruals, indicating cash flow reliability.

The company has successfully reduced net debt from £93.5m to £57.9m recently, helped by a share capital increase from £71.2m to £110.8m, reflecting a capital raise that strengthens the balance sheet. Cash balances have improved, and tangible book value is solid at £72.7m.

Valuation multiples are mixed: extremely high EV/EBITDA and EV/EBIT multiples reflect depressed earnings or market skepticism, while the free cash flow yield is strong at 26.9%, suggesting potential undervaluation on a cash flow basis.

## Conclusion

XP Power faces significant operational and financial challenges, with weak profitability and high leverage posing risks.
