### Analysis

This high free-float typically supports liquidity in the stock and reduces the risk of price manipulation by a few large holders.

Other significant holders include Van Lanschot Kempen Investment Management NV and STEEL PARTNERS HOLDINGS L.P., each holding between 7.5% and 9.5%. However, the presence of several activist or value-oriented funds (e.g., STEEL PARTNERS) may indicate pressure on management to improve operational performance or strategic direction.
