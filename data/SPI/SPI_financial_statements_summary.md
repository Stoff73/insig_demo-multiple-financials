## Alternative Performance Measures

### Definitions

**Adjusted EBITDA**
Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, amortization and exceptional items

**Adjusted EBIT**
Earnings before interest, tax and exceptional items

**ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)**
Adjusted EBIT divided by average capital employed

**Net debt**
Total borrowings less cash and cash equivalents

**Free cash flow**
Operating cash flow less capital expenditure

**Cash conversion**
Operating cash flow as a percentage of adjusted EBITDA

**Like-for-like/Comparable basis**
Excluding the impact of acquisitions and disposals
