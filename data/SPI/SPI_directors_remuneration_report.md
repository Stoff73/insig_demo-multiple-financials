# Directors' Remuneration Report

### Remuneration Policy Summary

**Base salary**
- Competitive market positioning
- Annual review based on performance
- Benchmarked against FTSE 250

**Annual bonus**
- Maximum: 150% of salary
- Financial targets: 70%
- Strategic/personal objectives: 30%
- 33% deferred into shares for 2 years

**Long-term incentives (LTIP)**
- Annual awards: 200% of salary maximum
- Performance period: 3 years
- Measures: EPS (40%), ROCE (30%), TSR (30%)
- 2-year post-vesting holding period

### 2024 Remuneration Outcomes

- CFO: 200% of salary
