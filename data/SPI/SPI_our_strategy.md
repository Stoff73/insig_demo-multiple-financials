# Our Strategy

### Our five strategic pillars

#### 1. Driving hospital performance

**Objectives:**
- Transform care, quality and service through centralisation and digitalisation
- Increase private revenue and optimize payor mix
- Maximize capacity utilization and operational efficiency
- Implement patient support centres for improved service

**2024 Progress:**
- Invested in hospitals business transformation
- Increased private revenue by 3.7% to £995.2 million
- £112.1 million capex investment across estate
- Opened new patient support centres in Cardiff and Seaham

**Key initiatives:**
- Digital transformation program
- Centralized scheduling and administrative functions
- Enhanced data analytics capabilities
- Infrastructure modernization

#### 2. Building on quality

**Objectives:**
- Achieve 100% of locations rated 'Good' or 'Outstanding'
- Implement NHS England Patient Safety Incident Response Framework (PSIRF)
- Develop clinical excellence programs
- Strengthen nursing and allied health professional strategies

**2024 Progress:**
- 98% of inspected locations rated 'Good' or 'Outstanding'
- Full PSIRF implementation across all hospital sites
- Launched Driving Clinical Excellence in Practice (DCEP) program
- Delivered 8 DAISY and 23 IRIS awards for excellence

**Quality metrics:**
- 97% patient satisfaction in hospitals
- 94% satisfaction in NHS talking therapies
- 84% of consultants rate care as 'excellent' or 'very good'

#### 3. Investing in our workforce

**Objectives:**
- Improve recruitment and retention
- Develop comprehensive reward framework
- Enhance training and development programs
- Reduce agency dependency

**2024 Progress:**
- New reward framework implemented in hospitals
- Retention rate improved to 86.1%
- Over 110 apprenticeship graduates
- 76% colleague engagement (83% response rate)

**Workforce statistics:**
- 17,600 total colleagues
- 380+ apprentices across the group
- Record levels of permanent employment in hospitals
- Lowest vacancy rates in recent years

#### 4. Championing sustainability

**Environmental targets:**
- Achieve net zero for Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030
- Increase recycling rates
- Reduce energy consumption
- Implement renewable energy solutions

**2024 Progress:**
- £10.2 million invested in solar panels and building management systems
- 31.4% dry mixed waste recycled (up from 23.5%)
- 2,742 tCO2e saved through waste management
- 54.7% female representation in senior leadership

**Sustainability initiatives:**
- 12,000+ solar panels installed
- Electric vehicle charging infrastructure
- Green travel plans
- Community engagement programs

#### 5. Expanding our proposition

**Growth areas:**
- Primary care services expansion
- New diagnostic and outpatient clinics
- NHS contract growth
- Occupational health services

**2024 Progress:**
- Three new clinics opened (Abergele, Harrogate, Norwich)
- Major NHS talking therapies contracts won
- New occupational health contracts secured
- Strategic partnerships developed

**Service expansion:**
- 276,500 people cared for by Vita Health Group
- 96,900 private GP consultations
- 800+ corporate clients for occupational health
