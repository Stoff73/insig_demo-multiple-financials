### Analysis Summary

**Valuation Ratios:** STV Group trades at very low valuation multiples such as P/E of 2.6x and EV/EBIT of 5.9x, suggesting undervaluation relative to earnings and operating profit. However, the EV/Revenue multiple is extremely low at 0.3x, flagged as a fail, indicating the market may have concerns about revenue quality or sustainability. Price to free cash flow is also low, which could reflect risk or market skepticism.

**Profitability:** The company shows strong gross margin (45.6%) and positive net margin (5.7%), indicating profitable operations. Operating margin is moderate at 8.9%, flagged as monitor, suggesting room for margin improvement. ROE is deeply negative (-167.4%) due to negative equity, which is a red flag and not sustainable. ROCE is healthy at 17.4%, showing efficient use of capital excluding equity distortions.

**Liquidity:** Current ratio at 1.5x is borderline adequate, quick ratio at 1.0x is sufficient, indicating the company can meet short-term obligations but liquidity should be monitored.

**Leverage:** Debt-to-equity is negative due to negative equity, which distorts the ratio but indicates low leverage in absolute terms. Interest coverage is weak at 1.5x, flagged as fail, indicating risk in servicing interest expenses. Net debt to EBITDA at 2.9x is moderate but should be watched.

**Efficiency:** Inventory turnover is strong at 7.1x and days sales outstanding is low at 16 days, both indicating excellent working capital management and supporting cash flow quality.

**Earnings Quality:** Accruals ratio is low at 2.5%, EBITDA to free cash flow conversion is strong at 76%, and there is no gap between adjusted and statutory earnings, indicating earnings are cash-backed and reliable.

**Asset Quality:** Goodwill to assets ratio is moderate at 12%, acceptable. Capex to depreciation is very low at 0.0, which may impair future asset base and growth potential. Tangible book value is negative at -£33.2m, reflecting accumulated losses and balance sheet weakness, a significant risk.

**Cash Flow:** Cash conversion ratio is 0.8, slightly below ideal, flagged as monitor. Free cash flow is positive at £7.3m, a strong point for the company.

**Ownership & Governance:** The shareholder base is concentrated with significant institutional ownership, led by Slater Investments at 19.29%. The board has seen recent changes including CEO and NED resignations and appointments, indicating active governance. Voting rights are standard with no unusual restrictions. Dividends totaling 11.3p per share for 2024 indicate confidence in cash flow.

**Audit Quality:** The auditor issued an unqualified opinion with no material uncertainties. Key audit matters focused on revenue cut-off and acquisition valuation, both addressed satisfactorily. Materiality levels are appropriate and audit scope comprehensive.

STV Group plc presents a cautiously positive investment profile.
