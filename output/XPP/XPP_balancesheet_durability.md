## Detailed Analysis

### Valuation

XP Power's valuation multiples are significantly above typical acceptable thresholds. EV/EBITDA and EV/EBIT multiples at 67.6x far exceed the fail thresholds of 7.5x and 10x respectively, indicating the market prices the company at a premium that is not supported by current earnings. EV/Revenue at 0.8x is below the pass threshold of 1.0x, also a fail. Price to free cash flow at 3.7x is very low, suggesting the market expects future recovery or growth. The P/E ratio is not available due to recent losses. However, the free cash flow yield is strong at 26.9%, indicating the company generates substantial cash relative to its market cap.

### Profitability

Profitability metrics reveal operational challenges. Gross margin at 39.2% is slightly below the pass threshold of 40%, warranting monitoring. Operating margin is very low at 1.5%, and net margin is negative at -3.9%, indicating losses. Return on equity is negative at -13.2%, and return on capital employed is only 1.9%, both well below acceptable levels. These figures suggest the company struggles to generate adequate returns on invested capital.

### Liquidity

Liquidity ratios are adequate. The current ratio of 1.6 indicates the company can meet short-term liabilities comfortably. The quick ratio at 0.9 is borderline but still acceptable, showing reasonable liquid asset coverage.

### Leverage

Leverage is a significant concern. Debt-to-equity at 1.1 exceeds the fail threshold of 1.0, indicating high leverage. Interest coverage is extremely weak at 0.2x, showing the company struggles to cover interest expenses from operating earnings. Net debt to EBITDA is very high at 24.5x, far above the fail threshold of 3.5x. However, recent capital raising (share placing increasing share capital from £71.2m to £110.8m) and debt reduction (borrowings reduced from £108.9m to £76.8m) have improved the balance sheet.

### Efficiency

Working capital management is strong. Inventory turnover at 4.2x and days sales outstanding at 22 days are both within pass thresholds, indicating efficient inventory management and quick cash collection from customers. Inventory levels have been reduced from £71.1m to £59.5m, supporting this.

### Earnings Quality

Earnings quality is high. The accruals ratio is negative at -31.2%, which is a positive indicator of earnings quality (less accrual manipulation). EBITDA to free cash flow conversion is exceptionally high at 1158.3%, showing strong cash backing of earnings. There is no gap between adjusted and statutory earnings, indicating no earnings manipulation.

### Asset Quality

Goodwill represents 17.6% of total assets, a moderate level that does not raise immediate concerns. Tangible book value is strong at £72.7m. The capex to depreciation ratio is 0.0, which is low and warrants monitoring for potential underinvestment in fixed assets or product development.

### Cash Flow

Cash conversion ratio is excellent at 5.8, and free cash flow is positive at £35.3m, supporting liquidity and debt reduction efforts.

### Ownership Structure

The company has a high free float of 94.88%, indicating strong market liquidity and low risk of price manipulation by insiders. Institutional ownership is diversified among several asset managers holding between 2.96% and 10.84%, supporting good governance and oversight.

### Going Concern

The notes confirm the company has adequate liquidity and covenant headroom under both base and downside scenarios, with a committed revolving credit facility maturing in 2027. The directors have a reasonable expectation that the company will continue as a going concern.

### Auditor's Opinion

The auditor's review report states no material misstatements were found and the interim financial statements comply with IAS 34, supporting the reliability of the financial data.

## Conclusion

XP Power Ltd. currently faces significant operational and financial challenges. Profitability is weak with negative net margins and poor returns on equity and capital employed. Leverage is high with poor interest coverage, posing financial risk. Valuation multiples are inflated relative to current earnings and cash flow, suggesting market expectations of recovery that are not yet realized.

However, the company demonstrates strong cash flow generation, efficient working capital management, and a solid ownership structure with diversified institutional investors. Recent capital raising and debt reduction have improved financial resilience. Liquidity is adequate, and the going concern assumption is supported by management’s forecasts and covenant compliance.

Investors should exercise caution given the weak profitability and high leverage. The strong cash flow and liquidity provide a buffer, but the risk of earnings volatility and financial stress remains. Monitoring profitability improvements and leverage reduction will be critical.
