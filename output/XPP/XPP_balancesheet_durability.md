## Analysis Summary

**Valuation:** XP Power's valuation metrics present a conflicting picture. The extremely high EV/EBITDA and EV/EBIT multiples (67.6x) are well above acceptable thresholds, signaling the market prices the company at a significant premium relative to earnings, which is a fail. The Price to Free Cash Flow ratio is also poor at 3.7x, failing the threshold. However, the Free Cash Flow Yield is strong at 26.9%, a pass, indicating the company generates substantial cash relative to its market price. This suggests the market may be discounting earnings weakness or risks but recognizing underlying cash flow strength.

**Profitability:** The company exhibits weak profitability. Gross margin at 39.2% is just below the pass threshold, flagged as monitor. Operating margin is very low at 1.5%, and net margin is negative at -3.9%, both failing thresholds. Returns on equity (-13.2%) and capital employed (1.9%) are poor, indicating operational challenges and low returns on invested capital.

**Liquidity:** The current ratio of 1.6 passes, indicating adequate short-term liquidity. The quick ratio at 0.9 is slightly below the pass threshold but still in monitor range, showing some reliance on inventory for liquidity.

**Leverage:** The company is highly leveraged with a debt-to-equity ratio of 1.1 (fail), very low interest coverage of 0.2x (fail), and an extremely high net debt to EBITDA ratio of 24.5x (fail). This raises significant financial risk concerns and indicates difficulty in covering interest expenses from operating earnings.

**Efficiency:** Inventory turnover of 4.2x and days sales outstanding of 22 days both pass, indicating efficient working capital management.

**Earnings Quality:** The accruals ratio is negative at -31.2%, a pass, suggesting conservative earnings recognition. EBITDA to free cash flow conversion is very high at 1158.3%, a pass, indicating excellent cash conversion. The adjusted versus statutory earnings gap is zero, a pass, showing no significant earnings manipulation.

**Asset Quality:** Goodwill to assets ratio is moderate at 17.6%, a pass, indicating no excessive goodwill risk. Tangible book value is strong at £72.7m. The capex to depreciation ratio is zero, flagged as monitor, which may indicate underinvestment or timing differences.

**Cash Flow:** Cash conversion ratio is very strong at 5.8, a pass, indicating excellent conversion of earnings to cash. Free cash flow of £35.3m is positive and a pass, supporting the company's cash generation strength despite earnings weakness.

**Notes on Going Concern and Liquidity:** The company has a $140m revolving credit facility maturing in March 2027, with £78m drawn and £24m undrawn as of June 2025. Covenants were amended in May 2025 to a leverage ratio max 3.0 and interest cover min 3.0, tested quarterly. Base and downside scenarios show the company remains compliant with covenants and maintains adequate liquidity, even under severe downside assumptions. The company is exposed to foreign exchange risk due to USD-denominated debt.

**Auditor's Report:** The interim financial statements have been reviewed (not audited) by PwC with no material misstatements found, supporting the reliability of the financial information.

**Ownership Structure:** XP Power has a single share class with high free float (~94.88%) and significant institutional ownership by Aberforth Partners LLP (10.84%), Van Lanschot Kempen (9.5%), Steel Partners (7.57%), and others. This ownership profile supports good market liquidity and active institutional oversight, which can be beneficial for governance and strategic direction.

## Conclusion

XP Power presents a mixed financial profile. The company struggles with profitability and is burdened by high leverage, raising financial risk concerns. However, it demonstrates strong cash flow generation and efficient working capital management, which supports operational cash health. The market valuation is high on earnings multiples but low on free cash flow multiples, suggesting the market prices in expectations of future improvement or strategic value.

Liquidity and covenant compliance appear adequate with prudent going concern assumptions. Institutional ownership and high free float provide governance and liquidity benefits.

Investors should be cautious of the high leverage and weak profitability but may find value in the strong cash flow generation. Monitoring operational improvements and leverage reduction will be critical to improving the company's financial health and investment attractiveness.
